# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Pensieve community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Report conduct concerns
privately to the maintainers through the Team AER GitHub organization. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
